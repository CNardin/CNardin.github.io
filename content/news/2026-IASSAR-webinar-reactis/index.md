> [!Info]+ IASSAR TC R3 Webinar
> Organized by the IASSAR Technical Committee on Robust Reliability, Risk & Decision Analysis (TC R3).  
> TU Munich and online (Microsoft Teams) — **29 October 2026, 5:00 PM (CET, Berlin time)**.

The IASSAR Technical Committee on Robust Reliability, Risk & Decision Analysis (**TC R3**) hosts a webinar on structural reliability and resilience in earthquake engineering, in which **Prof. Marco Broccardo** (University of Trento) will present our work developed within the [REACTIS project](/projects/reactis/).

🎤 **Speaker:** Prof. Marco Broccardo (University of Trento)  
📌 **Title:** *Integrating Structural Reliability and Resilience in Performance-Based (Earthquake) Engineering*  
🗓️ **Date:** 29 October 2026  
🕔 **Time:** 5:00 PM (CET, Berlin time)  
🎙️ **Chair:** Prof. Daniel Straub

📍 **In person:** TU Munich, Room N3620, Engineering Risk Analysis Group, Theresienstr. 90, 80333 Munich, Germany  
💻 **Online:** via Microsoft Teams (link in the organizers' announcement)

The webinar will explore the integration of structural reliability and resilience within performance-based earthquake engineering, an important topic for advancing the assessment and design of safer and more resilient engineering systems. Colleagues, researchers and students interested in structural reliability, earthquake engineering, resilience and risk analysis are warmly invited to join in person or online.
