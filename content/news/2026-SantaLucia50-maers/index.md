> [!Info]+ Rischio sismico nell'Alto Garda tra storia, prevenzione e pianificazione
> Technical conference with professional training credits.  
> Sala Fraglia della Vela, Via G. Maroni 2, Riva del Garda (TN) — **11 December 2026, 9:00–13:30**.

On 13 December 1976 the **Santa Lucia earthquake** struck Riva del Garda and the Alto Garda area. Fifty years later, under the patronage of the **Autonomous Province of Trento (PAT)**, a series of **technical and outreach days** is being organized to remember the event and to discuss seismic risk, prevention and planning in the area.

The technical conference on **11 December 2026** brings together the Province, research institutes and professional associations, with talks on the history of the 1976 earthquake, the geology and seismicity of the Garda area, local seismic response, structural retrofitting and the damage to the artistic heritage.

Within the [MAERS project](/projects/maers/), **Federico Ugolini and I** (DICAM, University of Trento) will present *"Vulnerabilità, esposizione e rischio sismico in Alto Garda"* in the second session.
