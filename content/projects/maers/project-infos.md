## Abstract

Italy sits on one of the most seismically active regions in Europe — yet comprehensive, high-resolution seismic risk maps at the local scale remain largely unavailable. **MAERS** (*Maps of seismic Exposure and Risk at regional Scale*) tackles this gap directly, developing the first Italian prototype of integrated seismic risk maps at a detail level unprecedented in the country.

The project rests on three pillars: **microzonation-enhanced seismic hazard** (capturing local soil amplification effects often missed by national-scale models), **data-driven vulnerability and exposure modeling** of the civil building stock (combining machine learning, advanced structural models, and field survey data), and a **publicly accessible web-GIS platform** for citizens, engineers, and decision-makers alike. The focus area is the **Alto Garda region** in Northern Italy — a highly seismic territory with a dense and historically rich built heritage — but the methodology is designed to be scalable and replicable across Italian territory.

MAERS is a pioneering effort in Italy, aligned with leading European frameworks such as ESRM20 (European Seismic Risk Model), and is the result of a multi-institutional collaboration between the University of Trento (DICAM), the National Institute of Oceanography and Experimental Geophysics (OGS), the University of Trieste (UniTs), and the Autonomous Province of Trento (PAT).

## Main Objectives

1. **Map the ground beneath us** — build a high-resolution seismic hazard model that explicitly captures local soil amplification using microzonation data and consolidated analysis techniques (NHV, GIT, EHV)
2. **Characterise what is at risk** — assess vulnerability and exposure of the civil building stock through structural models (SDOF) and machine learning, with dedicated fragility curves for critical facilities such as schools and hospitals
3. **Produce Italy's first local seismic risk map** — integrate hazard and vulnerability into a detailed, high-resolution risk picture of the Alto Garda region
4. **Make data accessible to everyone** — deploy an interactive web-GIS platform for both public consultation and expert use, enabling transparent and actionable communication of seismic risk
5. **Build a transferable framework** — design a methodology that is scalable to other urban areas and strategic infrastructure across Italy
6. **Support decision-making** — deliver operational tools for territorial planning, risk mitigation strategies, and emergency management
